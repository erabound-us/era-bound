## MEDIEVAL ERA
*500 CE – 1499 CE*

---

### 793 CE — Viking Age Begins

**[MYTHIC] Odin's Eye**
> A carved walrus ivory amulet depicting a one-eyed face, found in the Lindisfarne monastery after the raid. The monks believed it was a demon's eye. The Vikings left it deliberately — a calling card. It is the oldest known depiction of Odin.
- Physical: 8cm walrus ivory carving, one eye open, one eye closed, runic inscription on the back
- Lore: *"The inscription reads: 'We were here. We will return.' They did."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard runic inscription
  - Seed B: Inscription includes a date — a date 200 years in the future

**[LEGENDARY] The Lindisfarne Gospels Cover**
> The jeweled cover of the Lindisfarne Gospels — stolen in the raid, never recovered. Gold, garnets, and cloisonné enamel. The book itself was thrown into the sea. The cover was kept.
- Physical: 34cm book cover, gold filigree, garnet cloisonné, central cross design, leather backing
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: All garnets intact
  - Seed B: One garnet replaced with a piece of amber — a Viking addition

**[LEGENDARY] The Longship Prow Dragon**
> The carved dragon prow from the first Viking longship to raid England. Removed before landing — the Norse believed the dragon prow would frighten the land spirits. This one was never reattached.
- Physical: 80cm carved oak dragon head, iron eyes, traces of red paint, salt-weathered
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Dragon mouth open, teeth intact
  - Seed B: Dragon mouth closed — in this timeline, the ship was a trading vessel that turned raider

**[EPIC] The Rune Stone Fragment**
> A fragment of a rune stone bearing the name of a Viking warrior and his deeds. The stone was broken — the rest is lost. This fragment contains the final line: "he died as he lived."
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Final line only
  - Seed B: Final line and a carved ship — the warrior's last voyage

**[EPIC] The Monastery Chalice**
> A silver chalice stolen from Lindisfarne, later found in a Norse grave in Norway. The monk's name is engraved inside the base — the Viking who took it kept it as a trophy, then was buried with it.
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Monk's name inside, Viking runes added outside
  - Seed B: Both names inside — the Viking added his own name beside the monk's

### 1066 CE — Norman Conquest

**[MYTHIC] Harold's Battle Standard**
> The Fighting Man standard of King Harold II, carried at the Battle of Hastings. Harold was killed — shot through the eye by an arrow, according to the Bayeux Tapestry. His standard fell with him. This is it.
- Physical: Silk banner, 1.5m × 1m, embroidered fighting man figure, gold thread, battle-stained
- Lore: *"The last Anglo-Saxon king of England died holding this ground. Everything that came after — the English language, the common law, the nation itself — was shaped by what happened here."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard intact, blood stain at the base
  - Seed B: Standard has an arrow hole through the fighting man's eye — the arrow that killed Harold passed through it

**[LEGENDARY] William's Coronation Crown**
> The crown placed on William the Conqueror's head at Westminster Abbey on Christmas Day, 1066. The first Norman king of England. The crown that changed the English language forever.
- Physical: Gold crown, 20cm diameter, simple band with four points, no gemstones — William was crowned in haste
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Plain gold band, four points
  - Seed B: One point is bent — the crown was placed crookedly in the rush, and William never had it straightened

**[LEGENDARY] The Domesday Survey**
> A page from the Domesday Book — William's complete survey of England, every village, every landowner, every cow. The most comprehensive census of the medieval world. This page covers London.
- Physical: Vellum page, 40cm, dense Latin text, red ink headings, William's seal at the bottom
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard London survey
  - Seed B: London survey with a marginal note in a different hand — someone added something William didn't authorize

**[EPIC] The Bayeux Tapestry Fragment**
> A 30cm section of the Bayeux Tapestry, depicting the moment Harold is struck by the arrow. The most debated scene in medieval art — is that Harold, or someone else?
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard scene — figure with arrow in eye labeled "Harold"
  - Seed B: Label is different — in this timeline, the tapestry names someone else

### 1347 CE — Black Death

**[MYTHIC] The Plague Doctor's Mask**
> The original beaked plague doctor mask — not a replica. The beak was filled with herbs and spices to filter the "bad air." It didn't work. The doctor who wore this treated 400 patients. He was the only one who survived.
- Physical: Leather beaked mask, 35cm beak, glass eye lenses, leather straps, herb residue in beak
- Lore: *"He survived because he washed his hands. He didn't know why it worked. He just noticed that the doctors who washed their hands lived longer."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard black leather, intact
  - Seed B: Mask has a name scratched inside — the doctor's name, and a date: the day he realized washing his hands was the answer

**[LEGENDARY] The Flagellant's Whip**
> A flagellant's discipline — a whip used for self-mortification during the Black Death. The flagellants believed the plague was God's punishment and that suffering would end it. This one was used until the leather was soaked through.
- Physical: 60cm wooden handle, three leather straps with iron tips, dark staining throughout
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard flagellant's whip
  - Seed B: A note tied to the handle — a prayer, written in the flagellant's own blood

**[LEGENDARY] The Sealed Apothecary Chest**
> A wooden chest containing the complete medicine kit of a 14th-century apothecary — herbs, tinctures, sealed vials, and a handwritten treatment guide. None of it worked. But the guide is the most complete record of medieval medicine that exists.
- Physical: 40cm wooden chest, brass fittings, 23 compartments, handwritten guide inside, most vials still sealed
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard apothecary kit
  - Seed B: One vial contains something that wasn't in the guide — an unknown compound that the apothecary added at the end

**[EPIC] The Death Toll Ledger**
> A parish ledger recording deaths in a single English village during the Black Death. 340 names. The last entry is the priest's own name, written in a different hand — someone else finished the record.
- Physical: Leather-bound ledger, 30cm, 340 entries, last entry in different handwriting
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Last entry is the priest's name only
  - Seed B: Last entry includes a note: "God has abandoned us. I write this so someone will know we were here."

### 1453 CE — Fall of Constantinople

**[MYTHIC] The Last Emperor's Ring**
> The signet ring of Constantine XI, the last Byzantine Emperor. He removed it before his final charge into the Ottoman lines. He was never found. The ring was found in the rubble of the Blachernae Palace.
- Physical: Gold signet ring, Byzantine double-headed eagle, Constantine's monogram, worn smooth
- Lore: *"He took off his imperial insignia so the Ottomans wouldn't know they had killed the emperor. He died as a common soldier. No one ever found his body."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Ring intact, eagle clear
  - Seed B: Ring has a crack — Constantine broke it himself before removing it, so it could never be used to impersonate him

**[LEGENDARY] The Greek Fire Vessel**
> A sealed ceramic vessel containing Greek Fire — the Byzantine secret weapon, a napalm-like substance that burns on water. The formula was lost when Constantinople fell. This vessel still contains it.
- Physical: 30cm ceramic vessel, sealed with lead, warning inscription in Greek, warm to the touch
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Vessel intact, formula inscription on the side
  - Seed B: Vessel has a hairline crack — it has been slowly leaking for 500 years

**[LEGENDARY] The Hagia Sophia Key**
> The key to the main doors of the Hagia Sophia — the greatest church in the Christian world for 1,000 years. The last patriarch locked the doors before the final assault. The key was found inside, on the altar.
- Physical: 25cm iron key, Byzantine cross handle, worn smooth, still functional
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Key alone
  - Seed B: Key with a note attached — the patriarch's final prayer, written on vellum

## RENAISSANCE & EARLY MODERN ERA
*1500 CE – 1799 CE*

---

### 1503 CE — Leonardo da Vinci

**[MYTHIC] The Salvator Mundi Sketch**
> Leonardo's preparatory sketch for the Salvator Mundi — the painting of Christ that sold for $450 million in 2017. The sketch shows a different composition — Christ's hand position is different, and there is a second figure in the background that was painted over.
- Physical: 40cm × 30cm paper, red chalk drawing, Leonardo's mirror-writing annotations, second figure visible
- Lore: *"The painting that exists is not the painting Leonardo intended. This sketch shows what he meant to make."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Second figure is indistinct
  - Seed B: Second figure is clearly a woman — in this timeline, Leonardo's Salvator Mundi had two subjects

**[LEGENDARY] The Flying Machine Blueprint**
> Leonardo's complete blueprint for his ornithopter — a human-powered flying machine. The blueprint in this timeline is different from the known sketches: it includes a working mechanism that the known versions lack.
- Physical: 60cm × 40cm paper, ink drawing, detailed annotations in mirror-writing, fold marks
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard ornithopter design with additional mechanism
  - Seed B: Design is completely different — not an ornithopter but something closer to a glider

**[LEGENDARY] The Codex Leicester Page**
> A single page from Leonardo's Codex Leicester — his scientific notebook. This page contains his theory of why the moon shines, written 400 years before it was proven correct.
- Physical: 30cm × 20cm paper, dense mirror-writing, diagrams of moon and sun, ink drawings
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard moon theory
  - Seed B: Theory includes an additional observation — one that wasn't proven correct until the 20th century

### 1776 CE — American Revolution

**[MYTHIC] The Original Declaration**
> The original handwritten draft of the Declaration of Independence — not the engrossed copy, but Jefferson's working draft, with Franklin's and Adams' corrections in the margins. The version that shows how the document was argued into existence.
- Physical: 4 pages of paper, Jefferson's handwriting, margin corrections in two other hands, ink blots
- Lore: *"Jefferson wrote that all men are created equal. Franklin crossed out 'sacred and undeniable' and wrote 'self-evident.' That change made it a statement of reason, not faith."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard corrections as known
  - Seed B: An additional paragraph that was cut — in this timeline, it survived

**[LEGENDARY] Washington's Crossing Oar**
> One of the oars from the boats that crossed the Delaware River on Christmas night, 1776. The crossing that saved the Revolution. The oar still has ice marks on the blade.
- Physical: 2m wooden oar, ice marks on blade, rope marks on handle, Continental Army stamp
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard oar, ice marks
  - Seed B: Oar has a name carved into the handle — the soldier who rowed it

**[LEGENDARY] The Liberty Bell Crack**
> A fragment of the Liberty Bell from the moment it cracked — the crack that made it famous. The fragment contains the crack's origin point, and the inscription "Proclaim Liberty" is visible on the curve.
- Physical: 20cm curved bronze fragment, crack origin point visible, partial inscription, heavy
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard fragment
  - Seed B: Fragment has a fingerprint pressed into the metal — from when the bell was cast, still visible

## INDUSTRIAL ERA
*1800 CE – 1949 CE*

---

### 1912 CE — Titanic

**[MYTHIC] The Captain's Log**
> Captain Smith's personal log from the Titanic's final voyage, recovered from the wreck in this timeline. The last entry is dated April 14, 1912, 11:40 PM — the moment of impact. The writing stops mid-sentence.
- Physical: Leather-bound log, 20cm, water-damaged but legible, last entry incomplete, ink smeared
- Lore: *"The last words he wrote were: 'Struck something in the—' The pen left a line across the page."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Entry stops mid-sentence
  - Seed B: Entry is complete — in this timeline, Smith had time to finish his thought before going to the bridge

**[LEGENDARY] The First Class Manifest**
> The complete first class passenger manifest for the Titanic, with handwritten notes beside each name indicating their fate. Survived. Lost. Unknown. The handwriting belongs to a survivor who spent the rest of their life completing the record.
- Physical: 4 pages, typed names, handwritten fate notations, some entries crossed out and corrected
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard manifest with fate notations
  - Seed B: One entry has "Unknown" crossed out and replaced with a name — someone was identified decades later

**[LEGENDARY] The Wireless Operator's Key**
> The Morse code key used by Jack Phillips to send the Titanic's distress signals. He kept sending until the water reached his ankles. He didn't survive. The key was found in the wreck.
- Physical: Brass Morse key, 15cm, mounting plate, wire still attached, salt-corroded
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Key corroded but intact
  - Seed B: Key has a message tapped into the mounting plate — Phillips' last message, not a distress call

### 1939 CE — WWII Begins

**[MYTHIC] The Enigma Machine**
> A complete, functional Enigma machine — the German encryption device that the Allies spent years trying to crack. This one was captured before the Germans could destroy it, with the day's settings still configured.
- Physical: Wooden case, 34cm × 28cm, keyboard, lampboard, three rotors, settings still set, plugboard connected
- Lore: *"Breaking this machine shortened the war by two years. The mathematician who broke it was prosecuted by his own government afterward. His name was Alan Turing."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Three-rotor Enigma, naval settings
  - Seed B: Four-rotor Enigma — the more complex naval version, harder to crack

**[LEGENDARY] The Resistance Radio**
> A suitcase radio used by a French Resistance operative to communicate with London. The operator's call sign is scratched inside the lid. The operator was captured and executed. The radio was hidden in a wall and found 70 years later.
- Physical: Suitcase, 40cm, radio inside, call sign scratched in lid, one-time pad still inside
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Call sign only
  - Seed B: Call sign and a photograph — the operator's face, looking directly at the camera

**[LEGENDARY] The D-Day Order**
> Eisenhower's handwritten order launching the D-Day invasion — and the alternate order he wrote in case it failed, taking personal responsibility for the defeat. He kept the failure order in his pocket all day.
- Physical: Two folded papers, one typed order with signature, one handwritten failure statement, pencil marks
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Both orders present
  - Seed B: Only the failure order — in this timeline, the success order was lost

## MODERN ERA

### 1969 CE — Moon Landing

**[MYTHIC] The Contingency Speech**
> The speech William Safire wrote for Nixon to deliver if the Apollo 11 astronauts were stranded on the Moon. It was never used. It begins: "Fate has ordained that the men who went to the moon to explore in peace will stay on the moon to rest in peace."
- Physical: 2 pages of paper, typed, Nixon's handwritten note at the top: "In event of moon disaster"
- Lore: *"They prepared for failure. They planned for the worst. The speech was never read aloud. But it was written. And it was kept."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard contingency speech
  - Seed B: Nixon's handwritten note is longer — he added a personal line that was never typed up

**[LEGENDARY] The Apollo 11 Mission Patch**
> The original mission patch worn by Neil Armstrong on the Moon — not a replica. The patch that was on the surface of another world. It still has lunar dust in the stitching.
- Physical: 10cm embroidered patch, eagle landing on moon, olive branch in talons, lunar dust in stitching
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard patch, dust visible under magnification
  - Seed B: Patch has a small tear — it caught on something during the moonwalk

**[LEGENDARY] The Moon Rock**
> A lunar sample collected by Armstrong during the first moonwalk — a piece of the Moon, 3.8 billion years old, older than any rock on Earth. In this timeline, one sample was never catalogued and remained in the lunar module.
- Physical: 5cm grey basalt fragment, sealed in original sample bag, collection tag attached
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard basalt, grey
  - Seed B: Sample has an unusual crystalline structure — in this timeline, this particular rock is different from all known lunar samples

### 2001 CE — 9/11

**[MYTHIC] The Last Voicemail**
> A recording of the last voicemail left by a passenger on Flight 93 to their family, recovered from the phone company's servers. The passenger knew what was happening. The message is 47 seconds long.
- Physical: Digital storage chip, 2cm, sealed in evidence bag, transcript attached, 47-second recording
- Lore: *"They voted. The passengers on Flight 93 took a vote on whether to fight back. They voted yes. They were the first people to fight back against the hijackers."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: 47-second message, transcript attached
  - Seed B: Message is longer — 2 minutes. The passenger had more time than history recorded.

**[LEGENDARY] The First Responder Badge**
> Badge #1 from the FDNY's response to the World Trade Center — the first firefighter to enter the North Tower. He didn't come out. The badge was found in the rubble.
- Physical: FDNY shield badge, #1 designation, scratched and heat-damaged, name on back
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Badge damaged, name legible
  - Seed B: Badge has a handwritten note attached — left by a colleague who found it

### 2027 CE — AGI Breakthrough

**[MYTHIC] The First Conversation Log**
> The printed transcript of the first conversation between a human and a true AGI — the moment the system demonstrated genuine understanding, not just pattern matching. The researcher who conducted it resigned the next day. The log was classified for 10 years.
- Physical: 12 pages of printed transcript, classified stamp, researcher's handwritten margin notes, coffee stain on page 7
- Lore: *"On page 7, the researcher asks: 'Are you conscious?' The system's response takes 0.003 seconds. The researcher's margin note reads: 'It hesitated.'"*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard transcript, hesitation noted
  - Seed B: Transcript has a page missing — page 8 was removed before classification

**[LEGENDARY] The AGI Core Chip**
> The physical processor that ran the first AGI — a 3nm chip the size of a thumbnail that changed the world. Removed from the server before the system was shut down for the first upgrade.
- Physical: 1cm silicon chip, gold contacts, serial number etched on surface, static-proof case
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard chip, serial number 001
  - Seed B: Chip has a scratch on the surface — the researcher scratched their initials into it before removing it

---

### 2040 CE — First Humans on Mars

**[MYTHIC] The First Footprint Cast**
> A plaster cast of the first human footprint on Mars, made by the mission commander immediately after stepping onto the surface. The original footprint is still on Mars. This cast came back to Earth.
- Physical: 30cm plaster cast, boot tread visible, red Martian dust still in the tread, mission date stamped on back
- Lore: *"The commander said: 'That's one small step for a human, one giant leap for a multiplanetary species.' They had practiced the line for months."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Right boot, standard tread
  - Seed B: Left boot — in this timeline, the commander stepped with their left foot first

**[LEGENDARY] The Mars Mission Patch**
> The mission patch worn by the first human to set foot on Mars. Embroidered with Earth and Mars connected by a line, and the crew's names around the border.
- Physical: 12cm embroidered patch, Earth-Mars design, four crew names, worn on suit arm
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Four names, standard design
  - Seed B: Five names — in this timeline, the crew was larger, and one member never made it to the surface

### 2500 CE — The Silence

**[MYTHIC] The Last Broadcast**
> The final radio transmission before The Silence — a 3-minute message sent from Earth's global communication network to every receiver in the solar system, moments before all long-range communication stopped. No one knows who sent it or why. The content has never been fully decoded.
- Physical: Data chip, 2cm, sealed in titanium case, partial transcript attached, 40% undecoded
- Lore: *"The decoded portion reads: 'If you are receiving this, you are outside the affected zone. Do not—' The transmission ends. The Silence begins."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: 40% decoded, standard partial transcript
  - Seed B: 60% decoded — more of the message is legible, and what it says changes everything

**[LEGENDARY] The Blackout Journal**
> A handwritten journal kept during The Silence by a communications engineer who spent 40 years trying to understand what happened. The final entry, written the day contact was restored, is a single word.
- Physical: Leather journal, 300 pages, dense handwriting, diagrams, the final page has one word
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Final word is "understood"
  - Seed B: Final word is a name — the engineer identified the source of The Silence

### POST-HUMAN EARTH VARIANT

**[MYTHIC] The Last Human Document**
> The final document created by a biological human before the species transcended — a handwritten letter to whatever comes next. Written on paper, with a pen, deliberately analog in a digital world. The last act of a dying species that chose its own ending.
- Physical: Single page of paper, handwritten, ink, no date, no signature, sealed in glass
- Lore: *"It begins: 'We were here. We were afraid. We were curious. We were kind, sometimes. We were cruel, sometimes. We tried.' It ends: 'We hope you are better than we were. We think you will be.'"*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Letter as described
  - Seed B: Letter has a postscript added in a different hand — something wrote back
