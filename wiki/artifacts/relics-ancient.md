# Relic Catalog — Ancient Era
*10,000 BCE – 499 CE*

---

### 3,200 BCE — Early Dynastic Egypt, First Pharaohs

**[MYTHIC] The Narmer Palette**
> The original Narmer Palette — a 64cm ceremonial slate palette depicting the first unification of Upper and Lower Egypt. The most important object in Egyptian history. In this timeline it was never found by archaeologists — it was still in the palace when the multiverse transporter opened.
- Physical: 64cm slate palette, carved both sides, pharaoh smiting enemy on one face, procession on the other
- Lore: *"This is the moment Egypt became Egypt. One king. One country. One palette to record it."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard depiction — Narmer wears the white crown of Upper Egypt
  - Seed B: Narmer wears both crowns simultaneously — in this timeline, unification was instant, not gradual
  - Seed C: The enemy figure on the palette has been given a name in hieroglyphs — a name that matches no known historical record

**[LEGENDARY] The First Hieroglyph Tablet**
> A clay tablet bearing the earliest known hieroglyphic inscription — a list of linen and oil deliveries to a tomb. The first writing in Egypt was accounting. This is that tablet.
- Physical: 15cm clay tablet, incised hieroglyphs, fired hard, label tag still attached
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard inventory list
  - Seed B: The list includes one item that has no known translation — an unknown word in the oldest writing system

**[LEGENDARY] Pharaoh's Crook and Flail**
> The crook and flail of the first pharaoh — symbols of kingship carried for 3,000 years of Egyptian history. These are the originals, from the first dynasty. Every pharaoh after held copies of these.
- Physical: Crook: 50cm curved staff, gold and lapis lazuli banding. Flail: 40cm handle, three beaded strands
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Gold and lapis lazuli — standard royal colors
  - Seed B: Gold and carnelian — in this timeline, red was the royal color

**[EPIC] Faience Amulet of Horus**
> A faience amulet depicting the falcon god Horus, 8cm tall, glazed in turquoise blue. Worn by the pharaoh himself. The glaze is still perfect after 5,200 years.
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Turquoise blue glaze, falcon head
  - Seed B: Deep green glaze — in this timeline, Horus was associated with the Nile, not the sky

**[EPIC] Reed Boat Model**
> A 40cm model of a papyrus reed boat, fully rigged with a linen sail, found in a royal tomb. The actual boats that sailed the Nile in 3200 BCE looked exactly like this.
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Single mast, square sail
  - Seed B: Double mast — in this timeline, Egyptian naval technology was more advanced

### 2,560 BCE — Great Pyramid Under Construction

**[MYTHIC] The Architect's Master Plan**
> The original papyrus scroll containing the complete architectural plans for the Great Pyramid of Giza — dimensions, angles, internal chamber layout, everything. This document was thought to be lost forever. In this timeline, it was sealed in a jar in the architect's tomb.
- Physical: Rolled papyrus scroll, 2m long when unrolled, red and black ink, cedar wood case
- Lore: *"The Great Pyramid is aligned to true north with an accuracy of 0.05 degrees. This scroll explains how. The answer is more elegant than anyone imagined."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Complete plans, all chambers shown
  - Seed B: Plans with a chamber that doesn't exist in the real pyramid — a hidden room
  - Seed C: Plans annotated in a second hand — someone added notes after the architect died

**[LEGENDARY] The Pharaoh's Cartouche Ring**
> Khufu's personal signet ring — a gold ring bearing his cartouche, used to seal royal documents. The only object that can be definitively attributed to the pharaoh who built the Great Pyramid.
- Physical: Gold ring, 3cm diameter, cartouche of Khufu carved into the bezel
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Gold with lapis lazuli cartouche inlay
  - Seed B: Electrum (gold-silver alloy) — in this timeline, Khufu preferred silver

**[LEGENDARY] The Overseer's Ledger**
> A papyrus ledger recording the daily rations, work assignments, and names of the workers who built the Great Pyramid. This is proof they were paid workers, not slaves — and it contains 4,000 names.
- Physical: Rolled papyrus, 1.5m unrolled, columns of hieratic script, red ink totals
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: 4,000 names, standard rations recorded
  - Seed B: One entry is different — a worker listed as "from beyond the horizon," nationality unknown

**[EPIC] Diorite Statue Fragment**
> The head of a diorite statue of Khufu — the only confirmed portrait of the pharaoh who built the Great Pyramid. The complete statue is 7cm tall. This fragment is the face.
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard royal portrait, serene expression
  - Seed B: Expression is different — the mouth is slightly open, as if speaking

**[EPIC] Pyramid Alignment Tool**
> A wooden sighting instrument used to align the pyramid to true north using the stars. The tool that made one of the most precise structures ever built.
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Simple wooden frame with plumb line
  - Seed B: Frame with additional sighting holes — more sophisticated than any known tool from this period

### 1,350 BCE — Tutankhamun's Egypt

**[MYTHIC] The Golden Death Mask**
> Not a replica — the actual death mask of Tutankhamun. 10kg of solid gold, inlaid with lapis lazuli, carnelian, obsidian, and turquoise. The most recognizable object from the ancient world. In this timeline, Howard Carter never found the tomb.
- Physical: 54cm tall, 10kg solid gold, inlaid gemstones, striped nemes headdress, false beard
- Lore: *"He was nineteen years old. He ruled for nine years. He was forgotten for three thousand years. Then he became the most famous pharaoh who ever lived."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard mask — gold with lapis lazuli stripes
  - Seed B: Mask has been modified — a second face carved beneath the gold surface, visible only at certain angles
  - Seed C: The eyes are different — obsidian irises with gold pupils instead of the reverse

**[LEGENDARY] The Ankh of Aten**
> A solid gold ankh, 30cm tall, from the brief period when Tutankhamun's father Akhenaten imposed monotheism on Egypt. This ankh was made for the sun god Aten — the only god. It was hidden when the old gods were restored.
- Physical: 30cm solid gold ankh, sun disk at the top of the loop, rays extending downward
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Pure gold, sun disk prominent
  - Seed B: Gold with electrum rays — in this timeline, Atenism survived longer

**[LEGENDARY] The Canopic Chest**
> The alabaster canopic chest containing the four canopic jars holding Tutankhamun's preserved organs. Each jar is topped with a portrait of the king. The chest is sealed with the original wax seal, unbroken.
- Physical: 50cm alabaster chest, four compartments, four portrait-topped jars, wax seal intact
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Seal intact, all four jars present
  - Seed B: One jar is missing — replaced with a jar bearing a different face

**[EPIC] The Iron Dagger**
> A dagger found in Tutankhamun's tomb with a blade made of meteoric iron — iron from a meteorite, worked into a blade 3,000 years before the Iron Age. The most technologically advanced object of its time.
- Physical: 34cm dagger, meteoric iron blade, gold handle with granulation, rock crystal pommel
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard meteoric iron blade, slightly rusty
  - Seed B: Blade is perfectly preserved — in this timeline, the iron was treated with a technique that shouldn't exist yet

**[EPIC] The Throne of the Boy King**
> A miniature version of Tutankhamun's golden throne — 40cm tall, made for the tomb. The back panel shows the king and queen in a domestic scene, the only intimate royal portrait from ancient Egypt.
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard scene — queen anointing king
  - Seed B: Scene is different — king and queen are shown as equals, both seated

### 44 BCE — Julius Caesar Assassinated

**[MYTHIC] Caesar's Laurel Wreath**
> The actual laurel wreath worn by Julius Caesar on the Ides of March, 44 BCE. Found on the floor of the Theatre of Pompey after the assassination. Still bearing dried blood on the inner band.
- Physical: Dried laurel wreath, 22cm diameter, gold wire reinforcement, dark stain on inner band
- Lore: *"He wore it to hide his baldness. He was stabbed 23 times. Only one wound was fatal. The doctors argued about which one for centuries."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Laurel leaves mostly intact, gold wire visible
  - Seed B: Several leaves missing — torn off in the struggle
  - Seed C: A note tucked inside the band, written in Caesar's own hand — the content varies by seed

**[LEGENDARY] The Ides of March Gladius**
> One of the 23 daggers used in Caesar's assassination, identified by the blood on the blade and the senatorial ring found beside it. The owner never came back for it.
- Physical: 50cm Roman gladius, blood-stained blade, ivory handle, senatorial ring attached by cord
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Ring bears the seal of a known senator
  - Seed B: Ring bears an unknown seal — a conspirator history never recorded

**[LEGENDARY] The Veni Vidi Vici Dispatch**
> The original wax tablet on which Caesar wrote his famous three-word dispatch after the Battle of Zela: "I came, I saw, I conquered." The most concise military report in history.
- Physical: Wax tablet, 20cm, three lines of Latin in Caesar's handwriting, stylus still in the binding
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Three words, standard Latin
  - Seed B: Four words — an additional word that changes the meaning entirely

**[EPIC] The Rubicon Stone**
> A stone from the bank of the Rubicon river, marked with Caesar's personal seal. He picked it up when he crossed — the moment he committed to civil war. He kept it until his death.
- Physical: 10cm river stone, flat, Caesar's seal scratched into one face
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Single seal mark
  - Seed B: Seal mark and a date — the exact hour of the crossing

**[EPIC] Cleopatra's Correspondence**
> A sealed papyrus letter from Cleopatra to Caesar, written in Greek, found in Caesar's private quarters after his death. The seal is unbroken. No one has read it since 44 BCE.
- Physical: Rolled papyrus, 30cm, sealed with Cleopatra's cartouche in red wax, unbroken
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Seal intact, content unknown
  - Seed B: Seal partially broken — someone started to open it, then stopped

### 79 CE — Eruption of Vesuvius, Pompeii Buried

**[MYTHIC] The Pliny Letter**
> The original manuscript of Pliny the Younger's letter describing the eruption of Vesuvius — the only eyewitness account of the disaster. Written on papyrus, sealed in a lead tube, found in the ruins of his uncle's villa.
- Physical: Lead tube, 30cm, sealed with lead cap, papyrus scroll inside, ink still legible
- Lore: *"He watched his uncle sail toward the eruption to rescue survivors. His uncle never came back. He wrote it all down. This is that account."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard letter, two parts as known
  - Seed B: A third part — Pliny wrote more than history preserved

**[LEGENDARY] The Pompeii Bread Loaf**
> A carbonized loaf of bread from a Pompeii bakery, still in the pan, stamped with the baker's mark. The baker's name is known. He was found 10 meters from his oven.
- Physical: Carbonized bread loaf, 20cm, octagonal shape, baker's stamp visible, still in iron pan
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Stamp reads "CELER" — the baker's name
  - Seed B: Stamp reads a different name — in this timeline, a different baker owned the shop

**[LEGENDARY] The Fugitive's Jewelry Box**
> A wooden box, carbonized but intact, containing gold jewelry belonging to a woman who was found 50 meters away, still reaching toward the city gate. She dropped the box. She didn't make it.
- Physical: Carbonized wooden box, 15cm, gold earrings, necklace, and bracelet inside, latch still closed
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Three pieces of jewelry, gold
  - Seed B: Four pieces — a ring that doesn't fit any of the fingers of the woman found nearby

**[EPIC] The Gladiator's Helmet**
> A bronze gladiator helmet found in the gladiatorial barracks of Pompeii, decorated with scenes of combat. The gladiator who owned it was found in his cell — the door was locked from the outside.
- Physical: Bronze helmet, full face guard, embossed combat scenes, leather liner still present
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard gladiatorial decoration
  - Seed B: The helmet has a name scratched inside — and a tally of victories

**[EPIC] The Wax Tablet Diary**
> A set of wax tablets found in a Pompeii house, containing the daily diary of a merchant for the three months before the eruption. The last entry is dated the day before.
- Physical: Three wax tablets bound together, stylus attached, Latin text, last entry incomplete
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Last entry mentions unusual tremors
  - Seed B: Last entry mentions a dream about fire — written the night before the eruption

### 334 BCE — Alexander the Great

**[MYTHIC] The Sword of Alexander**
> Alexander's personal kopis — the curved cavalry sword he carried from Macedonia to India. Identified by the inscription on the blade: his name in Greek, and the words "given by Olympias" — his mother.
- Physical: 65cm curved kopis, iron blade, ivory handle, gold inscription on blade, worn leather scabbard
- Lore: *"He conquered the known world with this sword. He wept when there were no more worlds to conquer. He was 32 years old when he died."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Inscription reads "Alexander, given by Olympias"
  - Seed B: Inscription has an additional line — a curse on whoever takes the sword from his hand

**[LEGENDARY] The Gordian Knot Rope**
> The actual rope of the Gordian Knot — the legendary knot that could only be untied by the future ruler of Asia. Alexander cut it with his sword. This is the rope, cut end still visible, still tied in the original knot on one side.
- Physical: 2m rope, complex knot on one end, clean cut on the other, ancient fiber
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Cut is clean — one stroke
  - Seed B: Cut is ragged — it took more than one stroke, but history recorded only one

**[LEGENDARY] The Map of the Known World**
> Alexander's personal campaign map, drawn on papyrus, showing the extent of his conquests. At the eastern edge, someone has drawn a question mark and written: "what lies beyond?"
- Physical: Rolled papyrus, 1m unrolled, hand-drawn map, Greek annotations, question mark at eastern edge
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Question mark only
  - Seed B: Question mark and a rough sketch of something beyond — mountains, or a sea, or something else

**[EPIC] The Bucephalus Horseshoe**
> A bronze horseshoe from Bucephalus, Alexander's legendary warhorse. Bucephalus died in India after the Battle of the Hydaspes. Alexander founded a city named after him.
- Physical: Bronze horseshoe, 15cm, worn smooth on the ground surface, nail holes intact
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard horseshoe, worn evenly
  - Seed B: One nail hole has been filled with gold — a repair made by Alexander himself, according to the inscription

**[EPIC] The Philosopher's Letter**
> A letter from Aristotle to his former student Alexander, written after Alexander burned Persepolis. The letter is a rebuke — Aristotle tells Alexander he has become what he set out to destroy.
- Physical: Papyrus scroll, 40cm, Greek text, Aristotle's seal, Alexander's annotation in the margin
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Alexander's margin note is a single word: "wrong"
  - Seed B: Alexander's margin note is a paragraph — a defense of his actions

### 221 BCE — Qin Shi Huang Unifies China

**[MYTHIC] The Imperial Seal of China**
> The Heirloom Seal of the Realm — carved from the legendary He Shi Bi jade, bearing the inscription "Having received the Mandate from Heaven, may the Emperor live forever." Lost for 1,000 years. In this timeline, it was sealed in the First Emperor's tomb.
- Physical: 5cm square jade seal, carved inscription on base, dragon handle on top, deep green jade
- Lore: *"Whoever held this seal held the Mandate of Heaven. Every dynasty that followed spent centuries searching for it. It was here the whole time."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Deep green jade, dragon handle intact
  - Seed B: Jade has a crack — in this timeline, the seal was damaged when the Qin dynasty fell, and the crack is considered a sign of the dynasty's broken mandate

**[LEGENDARY] The Terracotta General**
> Not a fragment — a complete terracotta general from the First Emperor's army, 1.9 meters tall, with original paint still visible on the armor. Every soldier in the army was unique. This one was the commander.
- Physical: 1.9m terracotta figure, general's armor, original red and black paint, stern expression
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Standard general's pose, hands clasped
  - Seed B: General's hands are positioned differently — holding something that is no longer there

**[LEGENDARY] The First Emperor's Bronze Sword**
> A bronze sword from the First Emperor's personal guard, 91cm long — longer than any bronze sword should be able to be without breaking. The metallurgy is 2,000 years ahead of its time.
- Physical: 91cm bronze sword, chromium-plated blade (shouldn't exist), gold handle, perfect balance
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Blade is perfectly straight after 2,200 years
  - Seed B: Blade has one deliberate notch — a kill mark

**[EPIC] The Great Wall Foundation Stone**
> The first stone laid in the construction of the Great Wall, marked with the date and the name of the overseer. 300,000 workers built this wall. This stone was placed by the First Emperor himself.
- Physical: 40cm granite block, carved date and name, chisel marks from the Emperor's own hand
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Date and overseer's name
  - Seed B: Date, overseer's name, and a curse on anyone who removes the stone

**[EPIC] The Mercury Map**
> A bronze map of China from the First Emperor's tomb, with rivers represented by flowing mercury — still liquid after 2,200 years. The mercury is contained in channels carved into the bronze.
- Physical: 60cm bronze map, mercury rivers still flowing, mountain ranges in relief, sealed edges
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Mercury flows normally
  - Seed B: Mercury has formed a pattern that doesn't match any river in China — a map of something else
