# Relic Catalog — Deep Prehistoric & Prehistoric

> Relics are distinct from common artifacts. They are singular, culturally or historically significant objects — the kind of thing that would change a museum's collection or rewrite a history book. Finding one should feel like a discovery, not just loot.
>
> Each relic entry includes: the anchor it belongs to, rarity tier, a physical description, lore flavor text, and universe variants (how the multiverse seed shifts the object slightly between rooms).

---

## RELIC DESIGN RULES

- **One Mythic relic per anchor** — the most significant object from that moment in history
- **One or two Legendary relics per anchor** — objects of great power or cultural weight
- **Two or three Epic relics per anchor** — important but not singular
- **Rarity is fixed per relic** — unlike artifacts, relics don't roll rarity. A Mythic relic is always Mythic.
- **Universe variants change appearance and lore, never rarity** — the multiverse shifts the form, not the significance

## DEEP PREHISTORIC
*Before 3,000,000 BCE — Bucket: `deep_prehistoric`*

Relics here are primal — bone, stone, amber, and things that have no name because no language existed to name them. The multiverse variants mean some of these objects come from timelines where evolution took a different path entirely.

**[LEGENDARY] Homo Habilis Skull**

**[EPIC] Ochre Grinding Stone**

**[EPIC] Knapped Obsidian Blade**

### 3,000,000 BCE — Homo Habilis, First Stone Tools

**[MYTHIC] The Oldowan Core**
> The original stone from which the first intentional tools were struck. Not a tool itself — the source. Every flake removed from this core became a chopper, a scraper, a blade. This is the mother stone.
- Physical: Large rounded quartzite cobble, multiple flake scars, 15cm across
- Lore: *"From this stone came tools. From those tools came everything."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Quartzite, grey-brown, 12 flake scars
  - Seed B: Flint, darker, 8 flake scars — fewer tools made, each one more deliberate

**[LEGENDARY] First Bone Needle**
> A needle made from a bird bone, 8cm long, with a drilled eye. The oldest known sewing implement. Whoever made this understood that clothing could be constructed, not just wrapped.
- Physical: 8cm bird bone needle, drilled eye, polished smooth from use
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Eye drilled cleanly, needle intact
  - Seed B: Eye slightly off-center — the maker was learning

### 400,000 BCE — Neanderthal Peak, Cave Burials

**[MYTHIC] The Neanderthal Burial Crown**
> A crown of eagle talons and bear claws, arranged in a deliberate pattern and placed on the skull of a Neanderthal burial. This is proof — contested for centuries — that Neanderthals had ritual, belief, and ceremony.
- Physical: 12 eagle talons and 4 bear claws, sinew-bound in a crown shape, 400,000 years old
- Lore: *"They buried their dead with flowers and crowns. They grieved. They hoped. They were us."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Eagle talons dominant, bear claws at the front
  - Seed B: Bear claws dominant, eagle talons at the sides — different symbolic hierarchy
  - Seed C: Talons and claws alternating perfectly — mathematical precision

**[LEGENDARY] Neanderthal Flute**
> A cave bear femur with four deliberate holes. The oldest known musical instrument. Someone sat in a cave 400,000 years ago and made music.
- Physical: 30cm bear femur, four drilled holes, worn smooth at the mouthpiece end
- Lore: *"The notes it plays are a pentatonic scale. The same scale used in music across every human culture. Some things are older than history."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Four holes, evenly spaced
  - Seed B: Five holes — this version plays a different scale, one that doesn't exist in any known musical tradition

**[LEGENDARY] Cave Bear Skull Altar**
> A cave bear skull placed deliberately on a natural stone shelf in a cave, surrounded by smaller animal bones arranged in a pattern. This is a shrine. The oldest known religious site.
- Physical: Full cave bear skull, 50cm, surrounding bone arrangement, ochre markings
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Skull faces the cave entrance
  - Seed B: Skull faces the cave wall — toward the darkness, not the light

**[EPIC] Neanderthal Ochre Crayon**
> A stick of red ochre, 10cm long, worn to a point from use. Someone used this to draw on cave walls, on their own skin, on the bones of the dead. The oldest known pigment tool.
- Physical: 10cm ochre stick, pointed end worn, grip end flat
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Red ochre
  - Seed B: Yellow ochre — rarer, considered more valuable in this timeline

**[EPIC] Deliberate Burial Bundle**
> The grave goods from a Neanderthal burial — a flint blade, a handful of wildflower pollen (fossilized), and a small carved stone. Someone chose these objects to accompany the dead.
- Physical: Wrapped hide bundle containing flint blade, fossilized pollen, carved stone
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Pollen from seven flower species
  - Seed B: Pollen from one species only — a deliberate choice

### 40,000 BCE — Cave Art Explosion

**[MYTHIC] The Venus of the Multiverse**
> A carved ivory figurine, 11cm tall, depicting a female form with exaggerated proportions. Dozens of these have been found across Europe — but this one is different. It is carved from mammoth ivory that has been deliberately stained with a blue pigment that does not exist in nature. Someone in this timeline had access to something that shouldn't exist yet.
- Physical: 11cm ivory figurine, female form, stained deep blue, worn smooth at the base
- Lore: *"The blue pigment has been analyzed. It matches no known natural source from this period. The multiverse transporter log notes: 'anomalous material detected.'"*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Blue stain covers the entire figure
  - Seed B: Blue stain only on the face — deliberate
  - Seed C: Blue stain in a spiral pattern — the same spiral found on the Raptor Pack Bond Stone

**[LEGENDARY] The Lascaux Master Panel**
> A section of cave wall — 60cm × 40cm — removed intact, bearing a painting of a bull, a horse, and a human figure. The human figure has a bird's head. This is the oldest known narrative scene in art.
- Physical: Limestone slab, cave painting in black and ochre, 60×40cm, heavy
- Lore: *"The bird-headed man is falling. The bull is charging. The horse is running. This is a story. The oldest story ever told."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Bird-headed man is falling backward
  - Seed B: Bird-headed man is standing — in this timeline, the story ends differently

**[LEGENDARY] Bone Flute — Seven Holes**
> A vulture bone flute with seven holes, capable of playing a full octave. Someone 40,000 years ago understood music theory well enough to engineer an instrument with this range.
- Physical: 22cm vulture bone, seven holes, mouthpiece end worn smooth
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Seven holes, standard spacing
  - Seed B: Seven holes with a carved decoration between each — this maker was also an artist

**[EPIC] Hand Stencil Stone**
> A flat cave wall fragment bearing a negative hand stencil — paint blown around a hand pressed to the wall. The oldest known self-portrait. Someone said: I was here.
- Physical: 30cm limestone fragment, red ochre hand stencil, negative image
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Adult right hand
  - Seed B: Child's left hand — smaller, more tentative

**[EPIC] Carved Mammoth Ivory Bead String**
> 47 mammoth ivory beads, each drilled and strung on sinew. Each bead took approximately 45 minutes to make. This necklace represents 35 hours of focused work — the oldest known luxury object.
- Physical: 47 ivory beads on sinew, each 1cm, worn smooth from wear
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: 47 beads, uniform size
  - Seed B: 47 beads, alternating large and small — a pattern

### 10,500 BCE — Göbekli Tepe

**[MYTHIC] The Pillar Capstone**
> The carved capstone from the central pillar of Göbekli Tepe's oldest enclosure. It depicts a creature that is part human, part predator, arms outstretched — the oldest known deity figure. This pillar was buried deliberately 10,000 years ago. No one knows why.
- Physical: 80cm limestone slab, carved relief of humanoid figure with predator features, heavy
- Lore: *"They built the world's first temple. Then they buried it. Whatever they were worshipping, they decided it was safer underground."*
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Figure has vulture features
  - Seed B: Figure has fox features — in this timeline, the fox was the sacred animal
  - Seed C: Figure has no animal features — it is purely human, which is somehow more unsettling

**[LEGENDARY] The Enclosure Ring Stone**
> One of the T-shaped limestone pillars from Göbekli Tepe's outer ring, 2 meters tall, carved with a fox, a crane, and a scorpion in relief. Each animal is a symbol. The meaning has been lost for 12,000 years.
- Physical: 2m T-shaped limestone pillar, three carved animals, weathered surface
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Fox, crane, scorpion — the standard arrangement
  - Seed B: Fox, crane, scorpion, and a fourth animal that has been deliberately defaced

**[LEGENDARY] The Ritual Vessel**
> A limestone bowl, 40cm across, carved from a single block. The interior is stained with the residue of something that was burned in it repeatedly. Chemical analysis suggests: grain, animal fat, and a plant compound with psychoactive properties.
- Physical: 40cm limestone bowl, interior stained dark, exterior carved with geometric patterns
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Geometric pattern is angular — lines and triangles
  - Seed B: Geometric pattern is curved — spirals and waves

**[EPIC] Göbekli Tepe Flint Cache**
> A sealed clay pot containing 200 perfectly knapped flint blades, never used. They were made to be buried, not used. An offering to whatever was worshipped here.
- Physical: Clay pot, sealed with clay stopper, 200 unused flint blades inside
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: 200 blades, all identical
  - Seed B: 199 blades identical, one different — larger, made from a different stone

**[EPIC] The Buried Skull**
> A human skull found beneath the floor of Göbekli Tepe's central enclosure, with three deliberate cut marks on the crown. This person was not buried — they were placed. The cut marks are precise, ritual.
- Physical: Human skull, three cut marks on crown, ochre staining, wrapped in preserved hide
- Universe Variants:
  - Seed A: Three parallel cut marks
  - Seed B: Three cut marks forming a triangle — a symbol
