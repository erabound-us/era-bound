# Deep Prehistoric Era — "The Before Times"

## Multiverse Justification
In this timeline branch, the Chicxulub asteroid either missed, hit differently, or struck a world where evolution diverged. Dinosaurs survived and continued evolving alongside early hominids. The multiverse seed determines exactly which version of this chaotic, dangerous world players drop into. No two runs are the same — one seed might give you a jungle ruled by raptors, another a volcanic wasteland where a T-Rex patrols the artifact zone.

## World Variants (driven by universeSeed)

Each room rolls one of these world states. They share the same base map geometry but swap props, creatures, lighting, and artifact tables.

### Variant 1 — "Cretaceous Jungle" (65,000,000 BCE divergent)
The asteroid missed. Dinosaurs rule a dense tropical jungle. Early proto-mammals scurry underfoot. The air is thick, oxygen-rich, visibility low.
- **Dominant creatures**: T-Rex (patrol boss), Velociraptors (pack hunters), Triceratops (neutral, stampedes if startled), Pterodactyls (aerial threat)
- **Environment**: Towering fern forests, shallow inland sea edges, volcanic ridgelines
- **Hazard**: Raptor nests near artifact spawns — disturbing an artifact triggers a pack response
- **Lighting**: Amber-tinted, heavy canopy, shafts of light through breaks in foliage
- **Ambient**: Distant roars, insect drone, ground tremors from large creatures

### Variant 2 — "Volcanic Hellscape" (200,000,000 BCE divergent)
Pangaea is breaking apart. Massive volcanic eruptions reshape the landscape mid-match. Lava flows cut off routes. Early dinosaurs (smaller, faster) dominate.
- **Dominant creatures**: Dilophosaurus, Coelophysis packs, giant prehistoric crocodilians near water
- **Environment**: Cracked obsidian plains, lava rivers, ash clouds reducing visibility
- **Hazard**: Lava flow events — random map sections become impassable every 3 minutes
- **Lighting**: Red/orange volcanic glow, ash particle effects, no direct sunlight
- **Ambient**: Eruption rumbles, hissing steam vents, crackling lava

### Variant 3 — "Ice Age Crossover" (2,500,000 BCE divergent)
The multiverse seed produced a world where megafauna and early hominids coexist with cold-adapted dinosaurs. Woolly mammoths share territory with feathered raptors. Early Homo habilis camps dot the landscape.
- **Dominant creatures**: Woolly mammoth herds, feathered Deinonychus, giant ground sloths, cave bears, saber-tooth cats
- **Environment**: Frozen tundra, glacial caves, frozen rivers, hominid camp settlements
- **Hazard**: Mammoth stampede zones — crossing open ground risks triggering a herd
- **Lighting**: Cold blue-white, aurora borealis at night cycle, blizzard weather events
- **Ambient**: Wind howl, mammoth calls, distant hominid drums

### Variant 4 — "Swamp World" (300,000,000 BCE divergent)
Carboniferous period divergence. Giant insects, massive amphibians, and early reptiles in a world of enormous swamp forests. Oxygen levels are extreme — fires spread fast.
- **Dominant creatures**: Meganeura (giant dragonflies, aerial), Arthropleura (giant millipede, ground patrol), Pulmonoscorpius (giant scorpion, ambush), giant amphibians near water
- **Environment**: Enormous tree ferns, standing water everywhere, bioluminescent fungi at night
- **Hazard**: Fire spreads rapidly due to high oxygen — any explosion or torch creates chain fires
- **Lighting**: Green-tinted, bioluminescent accents, heavy fog over water
- **Ambient**: Insect chorus, water drips, deep croaking, wing beats

### Variant 5 — "Hominid Warzone" (3,000,000 BCE — closest to real history)
The most grounded variant. Early Homo habilis and Australopithecus tribes compete for territory. Megafauna roam. No dinosaurs — but the creatures are still deadly. This is the "realistic" end of the deep prehistoric spectrum.
- **Dominant creatures**: Saber-tooth cat (Smilodon), Dinofelis, giant hyenas, short-faced bear, terror birds
- **Environment**: East African savanna, rocky kopjes, dry riverbeds, acacia woodland
- **Hazard**: Rival hominid tribes — NPC groups that will attack players who enter their territory
- **Lighting**: Warm golden savanna light, dramatic storm weather events, night cycle with stars
- **Ambient**: Savanna wind, distant predator calls, hominid tribal chanting

## Creature Behavior Design

Creatures are not enemies in the traditional FPS sense — they are **environmental hazards** that create dynamic pressure on players.

| Behavior | Trigger | Effect |
|---|---|---|
| T-Rex Patrol | Enters Central Ruins zone | All players get audio warning. T-Rex destroys cover objects. Players must hide or flee. |
| Raptor Pack | Player picks up artifact in Creature Territory | 3–5 raptors converge on pickup location for 45s |
| Mammoth Stampede | Gunfire in open tundra zone | Herd charges in a straight line, knocks players down, drops held artifact |
| Pterodactyl Swoop | Player stands in open for >10s | Swoops and grabs — player is lifted briefly, drops artifact |
| Hominid Tribe Attack | Player enters Settlement without crouching | NPC tribe throws spears, alerts nearby players to your position |
| Lava Flow Event | Timer-based (every 3 min in Volcanic variant) | One map section floods with lava, forcing repositioning |
